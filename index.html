About Me

Cybersecurity meets Network Security

I am a Communications & Networks Engineer with a strong specialization in cybersecurity and network security. My technical background combines SOC operations, security monitoring, incident detection and response with network infrastructure, traffic analysis, firewalling, segmentation and secure network design.

During my final-year project at Innov Alliance Tech, I designed and deployed a complete cybersecurity environment integrating Wazuh SIEM, TheHive, Cortex, MISP, Palo Alto NGFW/IPS, Docker, VMware and security automation.

I also have hands-on experience with TCP/IP, VLAN, DNS/DHCP, VPN, NAT, DMZ, network traffic analysis, Wireshark, Linux, Windows Server and Active Directory. My objective is to contribute to secure, resilient and well-monitored IT and network infrastructures.
